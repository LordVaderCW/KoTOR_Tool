# Authors and Attribution

This attribution is based on `AssemblyInfo.vb`, `Licence.txt`, the release notes and the DeNCS manifest.

## KoTOR Tool

- **Fred Tetra** — original creator of KoTOR Tool. Assembly metadata also credits the original 2003–2005 work to TALII.
- **LordVaderCW** — 2026 restoration, repair and modernization maintainer; named as assembly company and restoration copyright holder.

The repository's source-available preservation license requires clear credit to the original author and restoration work. See `kotor_tool/Licence.txt` for the controlling terms; this summary is not a replacement for that file.

## Bundled and integrated work

- **JdNoa / Dashus / th3w1zard1** — authors listed in the DeNCS plugin manifest.
- **OpenKotOR/DeNCS** — external NCS decompiler project referenced by the plugin catalogs.
- **FMOD** — native FMOD Ex sound engine distributed as `fmodex.dll`; the repository should retain the applicable third-party license/provenance records.
- `FMODCL.dll`, `ImageTools.dll` and `RTFLinenumberTextBox.dll` are bundled binaries. Their complete provenance and licensing are not fully expressed in the current Markdown/source metadata and should be documented before redistribution or replacement.
